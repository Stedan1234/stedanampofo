Every post on this site is a Markdown file sitting in a folder called `content/blog`. To publish, I add a file and commit it. There's no editor, no admin login and no monthly bill.

That's a deliberate trade-off rather than laziness.

## What it costs

I can't publish from my phone, and I can't hand the keys to someone non-technical. If either of those mattered, a hosted CMS would be the right answer.

## What it buys

Posts live in version control alongside the code, which means history, diffs and rollbacks come free. There's no third-party service that can change its pricing or disappear. And the site builds to static files, so it's fast without any caching layer to reason about.

For a personal site where I'm the only author, that's the better side of the trade.
