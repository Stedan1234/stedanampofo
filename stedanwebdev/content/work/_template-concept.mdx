## The challenge

What's hard about this problem, and why the obvious answer doesn't work. Two or three paragraphs.

## The approach

What you decided, and why. This is the section that matters — decisions with reasoning, not a list of features.

## Decision one

State the decision, then the trade-off you accepted to make it.

## Decision two

Same shape. Three or four decisions is plenty.

## What I'd want to test

Concepts have no outcome data, so say what you'd measure if it were real. This is more honest than inventing a result, and it reads as rigour.
